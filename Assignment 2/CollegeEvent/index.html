Festival Overview

About InnovateX 2026

Empowering the Innovators of Tomorrow

InnovateX is Apex Institute of Technology's premier annual technical and cultural extravaganza. Designed to unite engineers, artists, coders, and performers from over 150+ universities across the nation, InnovateX offers a platform to showcase cutting-edge talents, compete for national glory, and forge lifelong connections.

Whether you are ready to debug code at 3:00 AM in our 24-hour Hackathon, crush the arena in Robo Wars, or rock the open-air stage in Battle of the Bands, there is a stage waiting for you.

Key Festival Highlights:

  • ✓ Industry-sponsored hackathons with direct hiring opportunities
  • ✓ Celebrity night performance & DJ extravaganza on Day 3
  • ✓ Free campus accommodation for outstation participants
  • ✓ Certificate of Participation & official merchandise kits

Guidelines for Participants

Follow these steps to ensure seamless entry and competition eligibility:

  1. Valid Student ID Required

    All participants must carry a valid physical College ID card during check-in.

  2. Pre-Registration Mandatory

    Complete online registration before October 20, 2026. On-spot entries are limited.

  3. Code of Conduct

    Maintain sportsmanship and strictly follow campus safety rules and event timings.

  4. Collect QR Entry Pass

    Present your confirmation email QR code at the registration desk upon arrival.
